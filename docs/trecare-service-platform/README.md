# TreCare Travel Services Platform

A responsive, production-style, zero-build UI prototype covering the complete TreCare service ecosystem.

## Included service modules

1. Visas & Insurance
   - Destination and eligibility form
   - Visa assistance tiers
   - Travel insurance comparison
   - Applicant document upload checklist
   - Review, payment, confirmation, and tracking

2. Passports & Documentation
   - New passport, renewal, lost/damaged passport
   - Name/address changes
   - Document attestation and translation
   - Applicant details and guided workflow

3. Airport Transfers
   - Airport, flight, pickup, and drop details
   - Sedan, SUV, and premium van selection
   - Passenger and luggage information
   - Review and payment

4. Forex & Travel Cards
   - Demo currency converter
   - Cash, travel card, and mixed forex options
   - Rate-lock and delivery workflow
   - KYC handoff placeholder

5. SIM & Connectivity
   - eSIM compatibility and activation dates
   - Data-plan comparison
   - QR activation workflow

6. Travel Assistance
   - Active ticket dashboard
   - Live support request creation
   - Booking, disruption, and document-help categories

7. Medical & Safety
   - Teleconsultation
   - Travel safety kit
   - Vaccination guidance
   - Medical declaration support
   - Destination health advisory

8. Lounge Access
   - Airport, terminal, date, and traveller inputs
   - Lounge comparison and selection
   - Included amenities

9. Meet & Assist
   - Arrival, departure, and transit support
   - Meet-only, fast-track, and premium packages
   - Special-assistance inputs

10. International Permits
   - International driving permit
   - Vehicle-border documents
   - Restricted-area permits
   - Document checklist and upload UI

11. Travel Essentials
   - Product catalogue
   - Add-to-cart flow
   - Cart modal and checkout handoff

12. Emergency Support
   - SOS activation
   - Medical, lost-passport, safety, embassy, legal, and stranded-traveller workflows
   - Saved emergency profile

## Shared platform capabilities

- Responsive desktop, tablet, and mobile UI
- Service catalogue and search
- Applications and orders dashboard
- Multi-step checkout
- Payment-method switching
- Confirmation state
- Application progress timeline
- Document upload states
- Service-management actions
- Cancellation request and refund estimate
- Support tickets and emergency escalation
- Toast notifications and modal workflows

## Run

Open `index.html` directly in a browser. No build process is required.

## Technology

- Vanilla HTML
- Vanilla CSS
- Vanilla JavaScript
- Lucide icons via CDN
- Inter font via Google Fonts

## Production integration points

Replace mock state and simulated actions with:

- Authentication and customer profile APIs
- Service catalogue and pricing APIs
- Visa/insurance eligibility engines
- Document storage and verification
- Supplier and partner integrations
- Forex-rate and KYC providers
- Transfer fleet and live tracking
- eSIM provisioning
- Lounge and meet-assist inventory
- Payment gateway and webhooks
- Notification service
- Support and emergency case management
- Invoice and document generation
