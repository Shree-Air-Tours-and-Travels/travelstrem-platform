How it works

A clear journey from partner to traveller.

TravelsTREM keeps the platform, product, agency, agent, trip and booking connected.

STEP 01

Agency joins

An agency applies and is reviewed by the TravelsTREM team.

STEP 02

Product access

Approved agencies are enrolled in eligible products.

STEP 03

Trips go live

Agency admins and agents publish and manage tours.

STEP 04

Traveller books

The traveller books with the responsible agency and agent.
